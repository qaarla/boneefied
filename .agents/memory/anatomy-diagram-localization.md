---
name: Anatomy diagram localization
description: Non-text localization boundary for licensed anatomy illustrations.
---

When activating Spanish study content, do not assume translated image metadata or verified label overlays make an anatomy plate entirely Spanish. Some plates include English lettering in the image pixels themselves.

**Why:** The source illustrations are licensed and medically meaningful; covering or replacing embedded text without checking rights and anatomy could misrepresent the source or obscure a structure. Static Spanish labels alone do not change what a learner sees in the image.

**How to apply:** Inspect plates that are actually displayed, distinguish baked lettering from separate UI labels, and provide a rights-safe Spanish presentation or explicit accessible companion text. Preserve English images in English mode and verify Spanish presentation offline on a native device.
