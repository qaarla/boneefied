---
name: Anatomy catalog counts
description: How to assess content completeness when composed content differs from authored module exports
---

Treat the assembled runtime catalog as the source of truth for content totals and lesson-level question coverage, rather than summing authored module exports or trusting a stored manifest alone.

**Why:** Composition adds visual/practical expansions and later lessons/questions after initial module definitions; a stored manifest once understated even the number of lessons in a published module. An apparently empty or thin area may be populated at runtime, while individual cards can still lack related questions.

**How to apply:** For future content audits, calculate counts and question-to-lesson structure overlap from the final assembled catalog; compare the manifest to that result and test displayed selector routes against the same catalog.
