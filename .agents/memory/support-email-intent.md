name: Support email scope
description: The owner's intended scope for Boneefied email branding and manual support replies.
---

Boneefied email branding is for manual support replies from
`help@boneefied.com`, not a new account-email system or automatic responder.

**Why:** The owner clarified, “I'm going to have to send it back” and
“It's only for the help@ bit.”

**How to apply:** Keep support reply branding consistent with the approved
website and existing logo. Do not broaden this into signup emails, marketing,
automatic support replies, or a new support application without a new request.
Only send a particular reply after the owner authorizes that send.
