name: Atlas control intent
description: The user's preferred visual hierarchy and compactness for controls beneath anatomy diagrams.
---

Keep the shared atlas controls compact and visually subordinate to the diagram. Preserve all five actions, Tinos, the Boneefied orange/charcoal palette, comfortable tap targets and centered, balanced labels. Allow text wrapping/reflow only when genuinely necessary with enlarged text.

**Why:** The user said Zoom in / Zoom out / Reset / Reveal all / Hide all consumed too much vertical space and looked oversized relative to the diagram, emphasizing “sizing, clipping, everything.”

**How to apply:** Treat compactness as a presentation goal, not a reason to remove actions, truncate labels or shrink tap targets. Keep the control block clear of images, legends, captions and bottom navigation.
