name: Rights presentation intent
description: The user's preference for module attribution placement and disclosure, without losing legal credits.
---

The user wants “Sources & rights” consistently presented as one compact, collapsed-by-default disclosure with a clear chevron near the bottom of relevant module/plate learning material, before final actions: “Sources and rights should be a dropdown menu” and “put it on the bottom.”

**Why:** The user does not want large always-expanded legal/provenance blocks interrupting the learning flow. Legally required attribution must remain available.

**How to apply:** Keep concise creator/license/modification credits with individual images when required, and put full sources, licenses, Source/Rights links, raw URLs, modification notices and detailed provenance behind the bottom disclosure. Preserve all actual rights data; never invent or weaken licensing information. Keep Tinos, the established palette/surfaces, expanded-state semantics and bottom-dock clearance. Preserve study descriptions and image-type distinctions; do not treat those as disposable rights boilerplate.

Put repeated image-type explanations and descriptive/specimen text behind a
compact, collapsed-by-default “Image details” disclosure globally. Preserve the
full wording when expanded and keep required concise image credits visible.

**Why:** The user said the repeated “2D diagram,” “schematic,” and microscopic
descriptions look like too many words and explicitly requested an expandable
dropdown “GLOBAL.” The user subsequently approved this collapsed disclosure
treatment.

**How to apply:** Use the same treatment in module galleries, lessons, glossary
illustrations and revealed Practice feedback. Preserve Recall and unanswered
Practice guards; expanding a disclosure must not disclose answers that the
current study mode otherwise hides. Do not collapse the actual structure label
list or change the diagram and photo-reference behavior under this instruction.
