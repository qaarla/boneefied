---
name: Rights presentation intent
description: The user's preference for module attribution placement and disclosure, without losing legal credits.
---

The user wants all module “sources and rights” to be a collapsed dropdown/disclosure at the bottom, after learning material and before final actions: “for all these sources and rights should be a dropdown menu” and “put it on the bottom too.”

**Why:** The user does not want large always-expanded legal/provenance blocks interrupting the learning flow. Legally required attribution must remain available.

**How to apply:** Keep concise creator/license/modification credits with individual images when required, and put full sources, licenses, links and provenance in the module's bottom disclosure. Preserve study descriptions and image-type distinctions; do not treat those as disposable rights boilerplate.
