---
name: Real-photo reference intent
description: Supplemental source-page links must preserve Boneefied's illustrations and distinguish verified counterparts from plausible candidates.
---

Real-photo references are supplemental source-page links, not replacements for
Boneefied's instructional illustrations. Preserve illustration quality,
numbering, existing provenance, questions and study progress. Do not copy,
embed or hotlink source photographs or thumbnails into the app.

**Why:** The user requested accurate, uncomplicated photographic references as a
separate addition while keeping the existing study material intact.

**How to apply:** Prefer legitimate human specimen sources, inspect the actual
photo and caption, and record the creator, relevant view, limitations and source
terms. Composite plates need specifically named references covering their
different subjects; a regional example alone is not complete coverage. Unknown
species, missing views and uninspected images stay explicitly partial or
unresolved, not approved fallback links. A human-anatomy textbook context or
Commons category alone does not establish a specimen's species. Linking rights
and reproduction rights are separate questions. Protect Recall and unanswered
graded contexts from answer leakage when adding links.

Treat a destination's caption as potentially revealing the whole plate. In
Recall, revealing only some markers does not authorize its photo link; complete
answer revelation does. In graded practice, marker interactions alone never
authorize the link before submitted/revealed feedback.

**Why:** One external specimen caption can disclose structures beyond the marker
the learner chose, so a partial reveal must not bypass the user's answer-leakage
boundary.

**How to apply:** Keep learning availability separate from assessment feedback
availability, including when future multi-photo references are added.

The owner wants coverage for **every published instructional image**, not
demonstration links or a representative sample. Maintain an individual outcome
and a specific reason for every unlinked image; never describe sample checks as
full-inventory verification or claim full coverage while gaps remain.

**Why:** The user repeated “for every image” and “Accurate. Not complicated.”

**How to apply:** Reconcile the current published inventory, pursue supported
matches individually, and distinguish direct matches, limited real examples and
incomplete cases in final totals.

Different donors and absent printed labels are not by themselves anatomical
mismatches. A genuinely suitable specimen can be a limited real example when its
views depict the represented anatomy, but unstated side must be disclosed rather
than asserted as the illustrated right side. Missing subjects or layers still
remain incomplete; a “real example” label is not permission to ignore them.

**Why:** These links supplement schematics with real anatomy, not identical
photographic reconstructions of the artwork.

**How to apply:** Use a minimum named set for genuine comparisons and disclose
material limitations in both languages. Never require proof of identical donor
identity just to show complementary specimen views.
