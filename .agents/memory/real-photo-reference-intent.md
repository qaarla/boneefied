name: Real-photo reference intent
description: Supplemental source-page links must preserve Boneefied's illustrations and distinguish verified counterparts from plausible candidates.
---

Real-photo references are supplemental source-page links, not replacements for
Boneefied's instructional illustrations. Preserve illustration quality,
numbering, existing provenance, questions and study progress. Do not copy,
embed or hotlink source photographs or thumbnails into the app.

**Why:** The user requested accurate, uncomplicated photographic references as a
separate addition while keeping the existing study material intact.

**How to apply:** Prefer legitimate human specimen sources, inspect the actual
photo and caption, and record the creator, relevant view, limitations and source
terms. Composite plates need specifically named references covering their
different subjects; a regional example alone is not complete coverage. Unknown
species, missing views and uninspected images stay explicitly partial or
unresolved, not approved fallback links. A human-anatomy textbook context or
Commons category alone does not establish a specimen's species. Linking rights
and reproduction rights are separate questions. Protect Recall and unanswered
graded contexts from answer leakage when adding links.

Use each illustration's own inventory and description to define its coverage.
Do not transfer a composite plate's missing subjects or paired-view requirements
onto a standalone tissue-type specimen just because both involve the same organ.

Treat a destination's caption as potentially revealing the whole plate. In
Recall, revealing only some markers does not authorize its photo link; complete
answer revelation does. In graded practice, marker interactions alone never
authorize the link before submitted/revealed feedback.

**Why:** One external specimen caption can disclose structures beyond the marker
the learner chose, so a partial reveal must not bypass the user's answer-leakage
boundary.

**How to apply:** Keep learning availability separate from assessment feedback
availability, including when future multi-photo references are added.

The owner wants coverage for **every published instructional image**, not
demonstration links or a representative sample. Maintain an individual outcome
and a specific reason for every unlinked image; never describe sample checks as
full-inventory verification or claim full coverage while gaps remain.

**Why:** The user repeated “for every image” and “Accurate. Not complicated.”

**How to apply:** Reconcile the current published inventory, pursue supported
matches individually, and distinguish direct matches, limited real examples and
incomplete cases in final totals.

Different donors and absent printed labels are not by themselves anatomical
mismatches. A genuinely suitable specimen can be a limited real example when its
views depict the represented anatomy, but unstated side must be disclosed rather
than asserted as the illustrated right side. Missing subjects or layers still
remain incomplete; a “real example” label is not permission to ignore them.

**Why:** These links supplement schematics with real anatomy, not identical
photographic reconstructions of the artwork.

**How to apply:** Use a minimum named set for genuine comparisons and disclose
material limitations in both languages. Never require proof of identical donor
identity just to show complementary specimen views.

Do not infer BlueLink gallery anatomy from its URL slug. A legacy
`deep-back-and-spinal-cord` page served anterior thorax/shoulder photographs,
while a differently named gallery contained the actual deep-back dissections.

**Why:** Legacy source routes can retain misleading names; blindly selecting a
region from the URL would attach anatomically incorrect reference links.

**How to apply:** Check the displayed photographs and embedded captions, and
retain the selected image-page associations. Collecting a gallery is not
evidence that every photograph in it was inspected.

Use efficient subject-grouped research rather than restarting with another
sample. Carry previous attempts forward, reuse inspected sources where accurate,
and pursue specifically missing structures/views through plausible new sources.
Do not repeatedly retry blocked or failed destinations.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires 164/164 individually verified accurate,
working real-life photographic reference sets, not a sample or a bounded-pass
completion claim. Concrete exceptions remain blockers, not permission to
approve partials or declare completion.

**How to apply:** Keep per-image outcomes separate from group research attempts.
Accounted-for is not the same as photographically covered. A slide overview
does not verify cellular details merely because the page describes them; keep
such composites partial. Do not copy, embed, hotlink, rehost, cache or add
thumbnails of third-party photographs as part of this work.

The owner explicitly authorized a new build on 2026-11-06 after being told
that all 164 illustrations have working companions but only 47 comparison
sets are complete and 117 are selected-structure examples. The request was
to make the hyperlinks clean, with simple text, and then create a build.
This overrides the earlier no-build restriction for that request only.
It does not authorize store submission, publishing, release scheduling,
or unrelated future builds.

**Why:** The owner's later explicit build request followed the disclosed
47/117 distinction; the earlier prohibition must not be treated as overriding
their current instruction, nor may approval be misrepresented as full coverage.

**How to apply:** Preserve the honest complete/limited distinction, follow the
existing EAS build runbook, verify exact source and artifacts, and keep building
separate from submission/publishing. Do not reuse this request as future
build authorization.

Having an inspected companion for every illustration is not the same as
having complete photographic comparison sets for every illustration.
Explicitly named, limited-view companions must remain visibly distinguished
from full-set approvals. Their presence alone is not build authorization;
the owner's later, explicit build-only request is a separate authorization.

**Why:** A count of linked illustrations alone could conceal missing subjects;
acceptance of a build must not falsely promote partial comparisons to complete.

**How to apply:** Report companion availability and complete-comparison
approval as separate totals, preserve the missing-subject research and
original inspection dates, and never approve a whole plate from source-level
inspection flags alone.

Distinguish a research-tool access or rate-limit failure from a destination
failure. A successful page-text fetch verifies metadata, not that its photograph
was newly seen. Reused inspection evidence must retain its original date.

**Why:** A collection page can remain publicly readable while the screenshot
service fails or captures only the introductory text. Treating either outcome
as a new image inspection would invent evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve prior genuine approvals, record the inspection
limitation separately, and do not promote newly found images from their titles,
attribution lists or metadata alone.

Use concise, learner-recognizable subject-and-view text for companion
hyperlinks rather than repeating “Real photo” or “dry skull.” Describe the
actual linked photograph, not whatever structure the question asks for.

**Why:** The user said the links can stay but “REALLY need to be clarified”:
generic repeated names did not explain their relationship to the illustration.

**How to apply:** Preserve destinations, opening behavior and coverage
disclaimers. A multi-photo group may have one unobtrusive sentence explaining
that its links show different parts or views. A labeling-only request does not
authorize new links, new source research or a redesign.
