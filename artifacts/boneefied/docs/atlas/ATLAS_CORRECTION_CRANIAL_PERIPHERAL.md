# Cranial & peripheral nerve visual audit

## Actual assigned scope

**14 individually opened visuals, 122 existing targets**, across Nervous System,
Skeletal System, Special Senses and Integumentary System. No new or duplicate
module was created. Seven Nervous System plates overlap the preceding review;
they were opened again for this group, not treated as uninspected or duplicated.

The associated plates are included because they actually depict named cranial
nerves, cranial exits, or cutaneous peripheral endings. Choroid plexus is not
mistaken for a peripheral nerve plexus; peripheral blood and the reproductive
pampiniform plexus are excluded. The neuron/glia schematic is not falsely
reported as a peripheral-nerve fascicle cross-section.

## What changed, precisely

- **Two source drawings/PNGs:** the two cranial overview plates. The additional
  V1 line and shared exit cues correct existing anatomy; no new structure,
  question, module or lesson was introduced.
- **One label/description-only plate:** smell/taste, three region-specific
  English/Spanish legend labels. IDs, ordering, coordinates and scoring remain.
- **Seven associated plates receive the same image-centered fit policy already
  used for the Nervous plates:** cranial floor, optic disc, eye section, ear
  section, inner ear, smell/taste and skin section. The other seven inspected
  Nervous plates already used it. No unrelated system-wide policy was changed.
- **Twelve source rasters are byte-identical** to their pre-audit files.
  Only the two corrected cranial previews get a new image-cache revision.
  No cache or saved study progress was cleared.
