## Anatomy limitation, not a certified repair

The retained 256×294 Servier pelvis assembles the femur and socket. The central
blue region does not reliably distinguish femoral head/cartilage from the
acetabular surface. Its point was moved off the central head/cartilage onto the
visible bony rim, but **the deep socket is still occluded and cannot be certified
from this image**. The existing original hip-bone detail already shows the
exposed acetabulum. No source reassignment, fabricated cavity or question change
was used to conceal this limitation.
