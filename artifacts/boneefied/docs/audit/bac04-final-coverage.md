## Exposure and honest limits

All 17 displayed Study selector choices resolve to a published module containing lesson cards, named searchable structures, and playable questions. Anatomy Foundations and Cytology/Mitosis are also visible in the unfiltered Learning modules list, despite having no dedicated system-filter chip. Search matches **structure names and aliases**, not lesson prose or disease names; it displays matches and allows bookmarking rather than deep-linking directly to lessons. Every published module has Practice questions using supported types; BAC04 questions are multiple choice and source-linked. Static catalog/route tests verify this; no native-device interactive run or build was performed for this content-only task.

The representative introductory structural syllabus is covered, including its change-with-aging/injury/disease objective. Remaining limits are explicit: text-only blood-smear identification cannot replace verified micrographs; the app does not substitute for dissection, specimens, or model-based lab exams; and the smaller endocrine/lymphatic modules have fewer practice items than the large musculoskeletal modules, although they are not empty. No clinical diagnosis or treatment scope is claimed.
