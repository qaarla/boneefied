# Generated anatomical background review

## Scope
Boneefied website only. No native-app, other-project, task-board or publishing changes.

## Artwork
- `public/assets/boneefied-dark-anatomical-material.jpg`: original generated artwork,
  created through Replit's available high-resolution image-generation workflow.
  A workflow specifically named “Image Imagine 4” was not exposed.
- `public/assets/boneefied-dark-anatomical-seamless.webp`: 2048 × 2048 mirrored
  raster repeat derived from the generated artwork, not traced or hand-drawn.
  Opposite edges matched before WebP encoding.

The generated material replaces the website's SVG masks. It covers the scrolling
shell, header, footer, workflow/access/support/coverage surfaces, local note,
filled action buttons and share controls. Dark charcoal, bronze and existing
amber accents are used regardless of operating-system theme. Tinos, existing
layout, content and behavior remain. Existing illustrative panels, logos and QR
interiors are not overlaid.

## Limitations
The artwork is decorative and nonliteral, not a scientific illustration.
The raster has deliberate repeat structure; this is not a unique illustration
for every scroll position. Readability was visually inspected, not formally
certified through a complete accessibility audit.
