Boneefied Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how the Boneefied anatomy study app and public website handle information in their current versions.

Information We Collect/Use

Boneefied currently has no user accounts, login, or cloud profiles. The app has no ads or advertising SDKs, GPS/location feature, photo or file uploads, or in-app payment processor.

The app uses your study input and preferences to provide practice and progress tracking. It also uses system appearance, screen and text-size information, and reduced-motion settings to adapt the interface. These are not sent to a Boneefied server by the current app.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message content, and any information you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not needed for your request.

Boneefied does not sell personal information. No personal-information sales feature or integration is present in the current product.

Local Study Data

Study answers, unfinished drafts, correctness and missed questions, mastery and progress, bookmarks, practice sessions, timestamps, and preferences are stored locally on your device or browser. They are not currently synchronized to a Boneefied server.

Preferences include theme, text size, haptics, question count, and language. The browser version may also cache educational images. These images are study materials, not photos uploaded by you.

Website/Service Providers

The public website uses Replit hosting and analytics. Visiting it may process basic traffic information such as IP and browser information, page URL and title, referrer, language, screen size, and country-level traffic information. This website traffic is separate from your locally stored study answers.

  • The website loads Google Fonts to display its typeface.
  • A QR image service at api.qrserver.com creates the website's QR code using the website link.
  • Some links open Expo preview/build pages or external educational and reference websites. Those services handle visits under their own privacy practices.

These services receive network requests when used. Their handling and retention of information are governed by their own practices; this policy does not promise specific retention periods for them.

The project's API records basic technical request information such as request identifiers, methods, paths, response status, and timing. It is not a destination for the app's study records.

Purchases

The paid Google Play download is purchased through Google Play. Google Play handles payment information; Boneefied does not receive your card details. The current app has no in-app payment processor or subscription feature.

Data Retention/Deletion

Local study data is kept in app or browser storage so you can return to it. In the app, open Settings and choose Reset local study data to clear study history, sessions and drafts, mastery/progress, bookmarks, and study preferences.

Language settings and browser image caches may be stored separately and are not cleared by that reset. You can manage remaining app or website storage through your device or browser settings. This reset does not delete support emails or service-provider records.

There is no account to delete. For questions about information you sent through support, email help@boneefied.com. No fixed support-message retention period or automatic deletion guarantee is stated here.

Children's Privacy

Boneefied has no account-registration or age-verification step. Local study data is handled as described above. If you believe a child has shared personal information through support, contact us so we can review the concern.

Security

Keep your device and browser access secure: someone with access to their storage may be able to access your local study data. No device storage or online service can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy does not claim that local study data is encrypted.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product or its information handling changes. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email help@boneefied.com.
