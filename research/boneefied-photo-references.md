## Executive summary

The runtime inventory contains 187 catalog assets. Of these, 164 are reachable through the lessons, galleries, glossary and question illustrations of 18 published modules: 158 diagrams and six existing histology assets. The remaining 23 unreferenced or superseded catalog entries are excluded. Each of the 164 published assets has an assessment; this is not a claim that each has a verified photographic counterpart.

The search assembled 120 distinct candidate source pages, opened 119 and visually inspected actual image content from 94. Many images are genuine and useful regional examples, but their sets do not establish the entire depicted subject/view of the corresponding composite plate. Other candidates have unconfirmed species, unavailable image inspection or incomplete source terms. Four records pass the conservative complete-counterpart criteria; 141 are partial and 19 remain unresolved. These totals deliberately exclude plausible but unverified links.

## Linking and rights

Ordinary outbound source-page links are distinct from reproducing, embedding or framing an image. The working policy records the guidance consulted from [Harvard](https://ogc.harvard.edu/pages/copyright-and-fair-use), [Stanford](https://fairuse.stanford.edu/overview/website-permissions/linking/) and [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia). No general guidance overrides a destination-specific restriction or guarantees that an uploader owns every right. The source creator, institutional provenance and stated reproduction license are recorded separately from linking guidance. This is not a legal opinion or a grant of image-reuse permission.

Histology Guide was excluded as a bulk source because its [collection terms](https://histologyguide.com/about-us/license.html) require permission for systematic retrieval into a collection or directory. No account, purchase, paywall bypass, unauthorized rehosting or remote image embedding was used. Source photographs were inspected temporarily; no new source photographs belong in the app or final research deliverables. Article quotations are not imported into the app.

## Important limitations and review corrections

- A diagram may combine whole-organ views, dissections and microscopic insets. A photo of just the gross organ does not verify that entire composite. Named references and missing subjects are detailed per asset.
- View and layer matter. A dorsal hand is not a palmar intrinsic-muscle view; an isolated bone is not a complete joint-ligament view. Missing panel coverage remains partial.
- Human identity is not inferred merely from a human-anatomy textbook or Commons category. The OpenStax spinal-cord micrograph was downgraded because its description does not establish specimen species. The mammalian simple-columnar specimen is likewise partial rather than an asserted human counterpart.
- The real paired femur photograph covers anterior/posterior aspects, but right-sidedness and individual panel orientation are not established. It remains partial, consistent with other laterality-limited records.
- Some source photographs are pathological, fetal or highly magnified examples. Those limitations are explicit; they are not silently presented as normal adult anatomy or the same schematic view.
- Twenty-three reference entries lack an established terms-page URL. They occur only in partial records and are not approved complete links.
- Rate limits and below-fold viewers blocked some image inspections. Those flags remain false; successful page fetches, captions or titles did not substitute for seeing the image.
- An unresolved outcome means no qualifying counterpart was established in this research pass, not that no such photograph exists anywhere.

## Authoritative files and implementation boundary

- `research/boneefied-photo-references/references.json`: all 164 per-asset records, with exact pages, creators, match descriptions, limitations, rights metadata and truthful visual/caption/page flags.
- `research/boneefied-photo-references/verified-references.json`: **only the four eligible records**. This is the allowlist for a subsequent link-only change.
- `research/boneefied-photo-references/summary.json`: actual counts, review corrections and explicit `coverageComplete: false`.
- `research/boneefied-photo-references/inventory.json`: assembled published-use inventory.
- `research/sources.json`: deduplicated candidate-page registry. A source having a genuine inspected image does not itself verify every plate referencing it.
- `research/boneefied-photo-references/evidence/`: short original observations and source metadata. Temporary screenshot filenames in working notes are inspection history, not retained photo assets.
- Group result files are working research inputs, not the final allowlist. Main review corrections in the authoritative files take precedence.

No illustration, numbering, existing provenance, question, progress behavior or visible interface is changed. No build or publication is performed. The later UI must not turn partial/unresolved candidates into fallback links, and must separately preserve Recall/unanswered graded-question answer protection. Full all-illustration photographic coverage is not ready.
