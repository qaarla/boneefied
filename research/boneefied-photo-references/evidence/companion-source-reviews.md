# Photographic companions: source-level evidence

These reviews establish what an actual human photograph shows. They do not turn
a selected-structure example into full coverage of a composite schematic.
Previous complete-comparison approvals and incomplete research statuses remain
separate. The app explicitly labels selected-structure references in English
and Spanish. A missing subject remains a full-comparison blocker.

Existing individually inspected human photographs are reused with their
original inspection dates and evidence paths, not presented as fresh
inspections. Only exact original source-page links are exposed. No source
photographs, thumbnails or screenshots are copied, cached, embedded or hotlinked
into the project or app. Recall and graded-practice revelation rules are
unchanged.

New source reviews are appended below as their photographs are inspected.
