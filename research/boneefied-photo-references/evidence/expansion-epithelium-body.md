# Epithelial comparisons and anatomical position

Inspected 2026-11-07. Photographs were viewed temporarily through their public
source pages. No source photographs, thumbnails or browser screenshots were
copied into the project or app.

## Human esophagus, nonkeratinized stratified squamous epithelium

https://tales.nmc.unibas.ch/en/histology-12/epithel-allgemeine-histologie-38/non-keratinized-stratified-squamous-epithelium-esophagus-218

The source explicitly identifies human child esophagus, H&E. All four static
photographs loaded. Main-agent inspection of screenshot `s2zv3t` confirms basal,
intermediate and flattened superficial nucleated cells; there is no surface
keratin layer. The comparison is tissue architecture, not an identical donor or
the schematic's perfectly regular cell shapes. Additional browser evidence:
`50uzjb`, `k6o3vz`, `16t4o8`.

## Human finger skin, keratinized stratified squamous epithelium

https://tales.nmc.unibas.ch/en/histology-12/epithel-allgemeine-histologie-38/stratified-keratinized-squamous-epithelium-finger-skin-219

Explicit human finger skin, H&E. All three static photographs loaded. Main-agent
inspection of `5x7nau` confirms the layered epidermis, conspicuous surface keratin,
epidermal/dermal interface and sweat duct. The browser separately inspected the
Meissner and subcutaneous gland/adipose fields (`vjjkqr`, `fsyvac`). This does not
independently establish every specialized epidermal cell type.

## Human trachea, pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium

https://tales.nmc.unibas.ch/en/histology-12/epithel-allgemeine-histologie-38/respiratory-epithelium-trachea-220

Explicit human trachea, Azan. All four static photographs loaded. Main-agent
inspection of `gn0mtg` confirms uneven nuclear levels, tall epithelial cells,
the ciliary border and goblet cells; the underlying collagen is blue. The
separate damaged field is not substituted for the intact epithelium. Other
browser evidence: `lri7r7`, `wf0sqx`. Cartilage was not independently established
in these captured static fields.

All three Basel overview viewers had high-resolution tile failures. The actual
verified evidence is the working static micrographs below them, as the bilingual
app labels disclose. The License sections identify University of Basel; linking
does not claim or exercise reproduction rights.

The fourth inspected epithelial candidate, bladder page 221, explicitly uses
**cat** tissue. Its images are not approved as human bladder evidence.

## Adult anatomical position, anterior and posterior

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Human_Body.jpg

Main-agent source-page screenshot `Human_Body_EMXj` shows four actual photographs:
adult female and male anterior views above and posterior views below, upright,
arms slightly abducted and palms anterior. The source explicitly identifies
photographed adult humans, studio photography with retouching/body-hair removal,
and model consent for the stated publication. The same source was inspected in
larger anterior-only form at `File:Human.jpg` (`Human_XFVD`), but the four-view
source is linked to avoid a second redundant destination.

The author/source record credits City Studios, Stockholm; uploaded by Mikael
Häggström, derivative assembly by Lamilli. The file states CC0 1.0 and includes
a separate personality-rights warning. No model photograph is reproduced or
embedded. Bilingual view labels identify the upper versus lower photographs.
This supports external anatomical position, not internal organs or body cavities.

## Three-type simple-epithelium comparison

The individually approved human squamous, cuboidal and columnar specimens are
reused with their original source-inspection dates. This comparison requires
those three epithelial forms, not all layers/cell types of a composite bowel
wall. Their different sites, stains, donors and naturally irregular cells are
disclosed. The epithelial/connective-tissue interface is the light-microscope
comparison; no claim is made that these routine fields resolve basal-lamina
ultrastructure.
