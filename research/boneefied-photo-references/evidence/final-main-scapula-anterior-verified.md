# Anterior scapula source review

Checked 2026-11-06.

- Exact source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anterior_surface_of_scapula.jpg
- Creator/source: Anatomist90, own work. File-specific CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported.
- Main-agent visual inspection used the original image linked on the opened
  Commons page, temporarily outside the workspace. The photograph shows a
  naturally textured, porous, brown dry scapula on a dark surface, with a complete
  subscapular fossa, borders, angles, glenoid side, coracoid and acromion. The
  photographic specimen has added explanatory labels; those labels are not
  copied into Boneefied.
- The provider classifies it as a human scapula photograph. Material assessment
  is supported by the actual visible cortical and porous bone detail, not by
  the filename alone. Donor history is not established. No exact right-sided
  donor identity is claimed; the app explicitly says the side is unstated.
- Together with the separately reviewed posterior specimen it provides the
  anterior/posterior anatomical comparison, not two views of one proven donor.
- The prior Palica anterior candidate had less explicit physical-specimen
  provenance and was replaced rather than treating its title as proof.
- The separately discovered “Scapula - anterior view.png” was rejected: its
  page explicitly identifies a BodyParts3D/Anatomography computer model.
- This is a source-page link only. No photo, thumbnail, quotation or label text
