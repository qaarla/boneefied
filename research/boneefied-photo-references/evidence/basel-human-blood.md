# Human blood-cell photographic comparison

Date: 2026-11-07

Targets: `asset-bvis05-blood-smear-schematic` and
`asset-bvis05-leukocyte-types`, assessed separately against their descriptions.

Source:
https://tales.nmc.unibas.ch/en/histology-12/cardiovascular-organs-anatomical-microscopy-50/blood-smear-315

The public source explicitly identifies fresh human peripheral blood stained
with May–Grünwald–Giemsa. All eight static image elements loaded. One is a
comparison table and is NOT counted as a photograph. The seven other panels
are actual stained smear fields.

The main agent viewed the following actual browser screenshots:

- `gh65a6`: segmented neutrophils with pale cytoplasm among erythrocytes.
- `1fzfhx`: large monocyte with an indented nucleus and surrounding erythrocytes.
- `3emksr`: small lymphocyte with dense rounded nucleus and scant cytoplasm.
- `68hdil`: eosinophil with conspicuous granules/bilobed nucleus, a neutrophil,
  and small platelet fragments, all in the actual stained photographic field.
- `0ylx63`: dark coarse-granulated basophil, lymphocyte and neutrophil;
  the next photographed platelet field is visible below.

This supplies all five mature leukocyte types for the comparison illustration.
It also supplies erythrocytes, platelets and all five leukocyte types for the
smear illustration. They occupy several photographic fields, not one field
with the schematic's artificial relative cell frequencies and enlarged white
cells. Stain, donor and field differ from the drawing.

The overview microscope reported tile-loading failures; the verified content
is the static photographs below it. Both language labels state this. A rendered
comparison table alone would not have qualified these records.
